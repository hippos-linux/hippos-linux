### Netplay lobby

- There's a HippOS netplay lobby now. Press X on the system carousel or pick NETPLAY LOBBY from the main menu to see who's hosting, grouped by system, with chat on the right. It only shows games you actually have.
- Chat has a global room plus one per system. Spammy messages get slowed down and filtered, and you can hide players you don't want to see.
- When someone wants to join your game, you get asked first. You can turn on auto-accept if you'd rather not be asked.
- RetroArch netplay never actually started before. It does now, including relay, passwords, public rooms and spectating, and achievements hardcore mode switches off while you're playing online.
- Dolphin can host and join from the lobby, no mouse needed. The game starts once the other player has joined and has the same game.
- Azahar (3DS) hosts and joins through its own room server, Flycast uses its rollback netplay, and PPSSPP ad hoc games show up in the lobby too.
- Riverhorse games hosted from its own netplay panel are listed, and public Riverhorse sessions from outside HippOS show up in the lobby as well.
- Netplay docs are on the website, with a section on each system's page.

### Desktop

- Web apps live on the desktop now. Add a Web App makes any site its own app with its own sign-in, and it shows up in the desktop menu as well as Linux Apps. Web apps you made with the old Web App Manager move over by themselves, sign-ins included.
- The HippOS Store has its own icon, and Discover and GNOME Software stay out of the way on installed desktops.
- Desktop shortcuts don't show the little link and warning badges anymore.
- Switching between EmulationStation and the desktop is quicker, especially on handhelds.

### Handhelds (RK3326)

- Early support for 16 XiFan handhelds: the XF28, XF35H, XF40H, XF40V, XF45V, R36 Max, R36 Max2, R36 Pro, RF35H, RF40H, RF45V, DC35V, DC40V, DC45V, Mini40 and MyMini. Pick yours from the devices folder on the boot partition. These are untested on real hardware so far, so please tell me how they go. Rumble isn't working yet on the XF35H, XF40H, RF35H and RF40H.
- Handhelds with internal storage, like the XF45V, should now be able to start HippOS from the SD card. Again, not tried on one yet.
- The screen import works with the original card's .dtb from more R36S-style systems now, like the R36S Plus. Before, it only took one kind and the screen stayed black.

### Games and emulators

- New game ports: Commander Genius (Commander Keen), Fallout and Fallout 2 Community Edition, Jazz² Resurrection, OpenJazz, Sonic 3 A.I.R., Sonic Mania, and Sonic 1, 2 and CD. You need your own copy of each game.
- New MAME computers: the Sharp MZ-2200 and the Philips VG 5000.
- Lunaria (Android games) is now built for the Raspberry Pi as well, though I haven't tried it on one yet.
- Riverhorse maps players to the right multitap slots.
- Games keep your screen's own resolution unless you've picked a video mode. Laptop screens that only offered their native resolution were being capped at 1080p.

### Fixes

- A failing or fake SD card could take your whole game library down with it and leave EmulationStation restarting over and over with "we can't find any systems". Now the bad card just gets skipped, and if the library ever does come up empty, EmulationStation tells you and lets you reload instead of crashing.
- The HippOS card's own Tools partition doesn't get mounted as a games drive anymore.
- The Wi-Fi switch in network settings shows whether Wi-Fi is actually on, and HippOS no longer turns the radio on by itself at startup.
