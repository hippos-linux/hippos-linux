### Desktop mode

- Switching between EmulationStation and the desktop works reliably again. Since 0.6.0, EmulationStation could stay running on top of the desktop, leaving a black screen, a missing taskbar, or a desktop that never appeared.
- Returning to EmulationStation from the desktop is about 10 seconds faster.
- Fixed black patches over the taskbar and tray icons after the first desktop notifications closed.
- On machines with hardware graphics acceleration, the desktop now uses the GPU for compositing.

### Audio

- EmulationStation and the desktop no longer wait 15 seconds on startup on machines without HDMI audio, such as handhelds, laptops, and PCs using analog or USB audio.

### Diagnostics

- Uploaded logs now include graphics driver details.
