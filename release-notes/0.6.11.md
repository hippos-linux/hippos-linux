### Raspberry Pi

- HippOS now runs on the Raspberry Pi 4 and Pi 5, from one image for both. Everything below applies to the Pi as well as PCs unless it says otherwise.
- Colours use the full RGB range by default, so the screen is no longer washed out, and boot is quieter.
- Bluetooth works out of the box.
- Windows software runs through Wine with box64.
- Steam Link is in the Content Downloader. Steam shows up in EmulationStation once Steam itself is installed.
- Xbox, Xbox 360 and PS3 aren't offered on the Pi: the Pi 5 isn't able to run them.

### ROMs and storage

- When HippOS finds a network share with games, BIOS files or saves on it, EmulationStation asks whether to merge it, the same way it does for USB drives: Ignore this time, Ignore forever, or Yes, merge share.
- BIOS files on a merged share are used straight away.
- Saving to a folder that only exists on a read-only share now works (for example new PS2 saves), with the new files kept on your own storage.
- The Windows system loads quickly even when its folder holds a web browser profile or other deep folders.

### Games and emulators

- PS1 games start in under two seconds, and DuckStation's window no longer flashes up before the game. Controls are fixed: the buttons are no longer swapped and the analog stick works in every game.
- PS Vita keeps its firmware, games and saves in `saves/psvita`, and games already installed in `saves/psvita/vita3k` open directly too. Vita firmware in `bios/` or `bios/psvita` is installed for you, and Vita3K uses Vulkan by default.
- Controllers in emulators that read HippOS's controller setup (such as Vita3K, gsplus and vpinball) have A/B and X/Y the right way round and both directions of each stick.
- The scraper gets to work much faster when your media is on a network share.
- The bundled free games (2048, Anarch, Craft, Gong, MrBoom, The Powder Toy) have full descriptions, pictures and videos.

### Desktops

- Desktop environments install in the background with a progress notification, so you can keep using EmulationStation.
- Installed desktops can be uninstalled from the same menu. Only what the desktop added is removed.
- The desktop list is ordered from lightest to heaviest, with a note on how much CPU each needs and how big it is to install. MATE installs its core desktop.

### System

- Audio Output in System Settings lists your sound devices again.
- Volume changed with the volume hotkeys is remembered after a reboot.
- Apps installed on first boot show their progress, and EmulationStation asks once before refreshing when they're done.
